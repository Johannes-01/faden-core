Follow the slides in order, so the student can read the notes side by side with the deck.

- One `##` section per slide, headed with the slide's title. Merge consecutive slides that continue the same title (e.g. "… (2)", or animation steps) into one section. Skip the title slide and pure agenda slides, or cover them in one line. With several decks, work through them in the order given.
- Each section has two parts, each introduced by a bold lead-in in the notes' language:
  - **On the slide** (German: **Auf der Folie**): what the slide says and shows, explained in full sentences or bullets rather than copied. Explain diagrams and graphics too (what the arrows, axes or boxes mean), and concrete numbers where they help.
  - **Added in the lecture** (German: **In der Vorlesung ergänzt**): what the lecturer said about this slide beyond what's on it: explanations, examples, emphasis, warnings, answers to questions. If the lecturer skipped the slide or added nothing, say so in one short line; that tells the student the slide got little weight.
- Where it helps the slides connect, carry one running example through the sections. Prefer the lecturer's own example; otherwise use one of yours, labelled 💡.
- After the last slide, add the "Unclear in recording" section.
